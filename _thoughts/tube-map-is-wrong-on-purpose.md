Beck's 1933 diagram is wrong about distance nearly everywhere and is the better map for it. Riders want stop counts and interchanges, not geography.

The failure case is the fun part. People in central London will ride two stops between stations that are a four minute walk apart, because the map puts them far enough apart to look like a journey.
