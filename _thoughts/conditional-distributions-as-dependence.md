Correlation captures a narrow kind of dependence: linear association between variables. Could comparing conditional distributions, \(p(x \\mid y)\), provide a more general way to characterize how much knowing \(Y\) changes what we expect about \(X\)?

What would make this a useful dependence metric? How should it behave under transformations, and how does it relate to mutual information or other measures of statistical dependence?

*An open question about the geometry of dependence, not a proposed metric yet.*
