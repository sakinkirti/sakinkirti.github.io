A tiny playground

How much information survives noise?

Imagine a binary signal passing through a noisy channel: each bit has a probability p of being flipped. As noise increases, how much information can the channel carry?

Channel capacity: 0.531 bits per bit

For a binary symmetric channel, capacity is (1 - H_2(p)), where (H_2) is binary entropy.
